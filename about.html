What We Teach

From Basic to Advance, we cover all traditional and modern Navratri styles in Surat!

Traditional Garba

Master the authentic Gujarati roots. We start from the absolute basics and teach proper footwork and hand coordination for Sada Garba, Be Tali (2 Tali), and the classic Tran Tali (3 Tali).

Surti Dodhiya

Feel the adrenaline! Learn Surat's famous high-speed, synchronized Dodhiya steps. From basic formations to advanced rolling and spinning Dodhiya variations.

Dandiya & Dakhla

Elevate your energy with powerful folk beats. We provide expert training in rhythmic Dandiya Raas, aggressive and energetic Dakhla steps, and popular group forms.
