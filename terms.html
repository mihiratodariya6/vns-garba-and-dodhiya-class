Navratri Preparation Guide 📝

Top 5 Garba Steps for Beginners

If you have never danced before, don't worry! Start with the simple Hitch, 3-Taali, and basic Dodhiya. These steps are easy to master and look incredibly graceful on slow traditional Gujarati songs.

How to Build Stamina for Navratri

Dancing non-stop for 4 hours requires immense energy. Hydration, learning the correct breathing techniques during high-bass Dakla beats, and doing warm-up exercises is the key to surviving all 9 nights.

What are the different types of Garba styles?

The most popular styles include 2-Taali (Be Taali), 3-Taali (Tran Taali), Dodhiya (Surat special), Hitch, Popat, and Dakla (high-energy trance). We teach all these variations in our offline batches and online programs.
